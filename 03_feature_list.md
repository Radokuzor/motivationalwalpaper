# motivationalwallpaper.com — Product Feature List

## Core product philosophy

The wallpaper is the top of the funnel — the door. Nothing more. The product is everything that happens after the user walks through that door: a daily engagement system that nudges people from who they are now toward who they want to become, delivered through widgets, push notifications, SMS, email, and community.

Personalization is powered by **preset customer profiles** selected at onboarding. Each profile routes the user to tailored content automatically. The six profiles are:

- **Marcus** — discipline, stoicism, gym, silent grind (male, 22–28)
- **Sarah** — aesthetic, self-improvement, glow-up, soft life (female, 17–22)
- **Diane** — rebuilding, healing, burnout recovery, faith (female, 30–42)
- **Pastor James** — faith leadership, scripture, ministry community (35–55)
- **Jerome** — entrepreneurship, side hustle, builder mindset (male, 25–35)
- **Grace** — aesthetic phone customization, teen self-esteem (female, 14–18)

## Delivery channels

### 1. Home screen widget
Passive, always visible. Seen 100+ times a day without opening anything.

- Profile-matched affirmation displayed directly on home screen widget
- Content refreshes on a schedule (not daily wallpaper — the affirmation/quote rotates)
- Streak counter visible at a glance
- One-tap from widget opens today's micro-action
- **Like button on widget** — user can heart today's content with one tap
- **Share button on widget** — one tap generates a beautiful branded image of the quote/affirmation with a designed background, ready to share to Instagram, iMessage, WhatsApp, or copy link

### 2. Push notifications
Timed nudges that meet them in the right moment — morning, afternoon, and night.

- Morning (7am): today's affirmation + micro-action prompt
- Midday (12pm): optional check-in ping ("did you do the thing?")
- Evening (9pm): one-line reflection question
- User chooses their notification cadence at onboarding
- **Notifications are engageable** — swipe actions: heart to save, share to generate branded share card
- Notification content is profile-specific (stoic quote for Marcus, scripture for Pastor James, abundance affirmation for Sarah, etc.)

### 3. SMS / text message
Highest open rate. Most intimate channel. Feels personal.

- Daily profile-matched quote sent as a plain text message
- Optional reply-based micro-journal ("reply with one word for today")
- Friday win prompt: "what did you prove this week?" — reply goes into growth log
- Content is short enough to read in under 5 seconds
- $4.99/mo subscription — stickiest recurring revenue product
- No links, no CTAs in the daily message — just the content

### 4. Email newsletter
Deeper weekly content. The format users save and return to.

- Weekly profile-specific content drop (not wallpapers — insights, prompts, audio links)
- 7-day micro-challenges delivered one email per day
- Monthly transformation check-in with user's progress data
- Exclusive content not available anywhere else
- Beautiful designed emails — shareable individual quote cards embedded in email body
- Platform: Beehiiv, segmented by customer profile

## Social engagement system (cross-feature)

Inspired by Locket's reaction and sharing mechanics, applied to transformation content:

- **Like / heart**: Available on every piece of content — widget, notification, SMS, email. Saves to user's personal favorites library.
- **One-tap share card**: Any liked or highlighted piece of content generates a beautifully designed image — quote text, profile-appropriate background design, motivationalwallpaper.com branding — ready to share to Instagram Stories, iMessage, WhatsApp, or copy as image. No friction. One button.
- **Share to friend**: Option to send directly to a friend via the app. Friend receives the content with a "sent by [name]" wrapper — organic referral loop.
- **No public metrics**: No follower count, no public likes. The engagement is personal and intimate, not performative. Inspired by Locket's deliberate choice to not count or display reactions.

## What this product is NOT

- Not a wallpaper subscription service
- Not a generic quote app
- Not a meditation app
- Not a fitness tracker
- Not a one-size-fits-all wellness tool

## What this product IS

A daily identity reinforcement system. It meets the user wherever they are (phone screen, notification, text, email) with content tailored to the version of themselves they are trying to become — and gives them one small, frictionless action each day that proves they're already becoming that person.
