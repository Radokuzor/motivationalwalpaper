# motivationalwallpaper.com — Audience Transformation Profiles

## How these profiles work

These are NOT marketing segments or content pillars. They are **preset profile types** assigned to each user during onboarding so the product can serve them tailored content automatically. The user takes a 3-question quiz after their first wallpaper capture. Their answers route them to one of these six profiles. Everything they receive from that point — notifications, SMS, email, audio, challenges — is calibrated to their profile.

The site itself does not label or target visitors by profile during browsing. The wallpaper reel is a TikTok-style vertical scroll of single full-bleed wallpapers, one per panel, alternating opposite worlds (Stoic → Soft Life → Scripture → …) so any visitor sees themselves within two swipes.

---

## The master transformation arc

Every profile shares the same core emotional movement:

**BEFORE:** *"I am not the person I want to be yet, and that gap is uncomfortable."*

**→**

**AFTER:** *"Every morning something here reminds me who I'm becoming — and I'm starting to believe it."*

The wallpaper opens the identity gap. The daily engagement system closes it — one morning at a time. The product is not motivation. It is the **daily experience of becoming.**

---

## The six triggers that send all profiles here

1. **The restart moment** — New Year, birthday, new month, moving somewhere new. Phone reset is part of a bigger "starting over" ritual. Peaks every January, September, Monday mornings.
2. **The rock bottom moment** — Breakup, job loss, health scare, confronting the gap between who they are and who they meant to be.
3. **The phone reset ritual** — Cleaning apps, deleting photos, reorganizing. Always want a new wallpaper. Repeats monthly for many users.
4. **The trend entry moment** — Winter arc, glow-up era, 75 Hard, vision board aesthetic on TikTok. Wallpaper = lowest-barrier way to visually participate.
5. **The community share** — Someone in a faith group, gym, or WhatsApp circle shared a wallpaper. One Pastor James reaches 200 people. Word of mouth inside communities is highest-converting channel.
6. **The comparison spiral** — Been scrolling. Saw someone who has what they want. Felt bad. Searched for something that pushes back.

## Profile quiz (post-capture routing)

After a visitor submits their email or phone number, a 3-question inline quiz routes them to their profile. No progress bar. No skip. Each answer uses identity language — "That's me" — not A/B/C/D.

**Question 1: "What does a good morning look like for you?"**
- Quiet and intentional — coffee, scripture, stillness → James
- Moving — gym, walk, first task done before 8am → Marcus
- Soft — slow start, journal, no rush → Sarah / Grace
- Locked in — review goals, set the day, execute → Jerome

**Question 2: "What's the thing you most want to feel more of?"**
- Peace and purpose → James / Diane
- Discipline and edge → Marcus
- Confidence and self-love → Sarah / Grace
- Momentum and progress → Jerome

**Question 3: "What's pulling at you right now?"**
- I'm rebuilding after something hard → Diane
- I'm building toward something big → Jerome
- I'm trying to feel like myself again → Diane / Sarah
- I'm trying to be who I know I'm capable of being → Marcus / Jerome
