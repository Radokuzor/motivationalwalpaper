## What the site is and is not

### What it is NOT
- A wallpaper subscription service
- A wallpaper drop platform
- A generic quote app

### What it IS
A **daily identity reinforcement system** that meets people where they are — on their phone screen, in their notifications, in their inbox, in a text message — with content tailored to the version of themselves they are trying to become, and one small frictionless action each day that proves they're already becoming that person.

### The wallpaper's role
The wallpaper is the **top of the funnel — the door. Nothing more.** People do not change their wallpaper daily. The wallpaper captures their contact and routes them into the engagement system. Everything after the download is about daily engagement through notifications, widgets, audio, SMS, email, and community.

## Product vision — the gap we own

Three existing products do adjacent things:

| Product | What they nail | What they miss |
|---|---|---|
| **Locket Widget** | Phone screen presence, intimate delivery, social sharing | Zero transformation intent, no personalization to identity |
| **Fabulous** | Habit stacking, daily routines, journeys, community circles | Generic identity, clinical feel, no visual anchor |
| **Calm** | Daily ritual, feels peaceful, sleep + audio content | No habit accountability, no identity system, no phone screen presence |

**The gap:** A daily visual identity anchor on the phone screen, tied to who you specifically are trying to become, followed by one micro-action that proves you already are that person.

Nobody owns this. The wallpaper is the entry point none of them have.

## Social engagement system

Inspired by Locket's reaction and sharing mechanics:

- **Like/heart** on any content piece — widget, notification, SMS, email — saves to personal favorites
- **One-tap share card** — generates a beautiful branded image (quote + designed background + site URL) shareable to Instagram, iMessage, WhatsApp
- **No public metrics** — no follower count, no public like counts. Intimate, not performative
- **Friend share** — send directly to a friend; they receive it with a referral wrapper → organic acquisition loop

## Traffic growth priorities

### SEO quick wins
1. Create dedicated landing pages: `/iphone-wallpapers/motivational`, `/iphone-wallpapers/christian`, `/iphone-wallpapers/aesthetic`, `/iphone-wallpapers/stoic` — domain already has authority for these exact terms, KD is low
2. Add FAQ sections to existing pages — feeds Google AI Overviews and ChatGPT citations directly
3. Build 3–5 comprehensive hub pages per keyword cluster — AI citation magnets
4. Gemini optimization — currently 0 visibility, structured content wins available

### Pinterest (biggest untapped channel)
- Motivational content is among the most pinned on Pinterest
- Auto-post via Tailwind — evergreen traffic, pins from 2 years ago still convert
- Target: 50 pins/week minimum
- Pinterest passes genuine SEO authority through profile links

### TikTok / Instagram Reels
- "New content" style videos — quote animating on a phone screen
- Consistent performers in the aesthetic and Christian communities
- Zero ad spend required early

### Content expansion (SEO long-tail)
Target keywords that feed the same audience at 10–50x higher volume:
- "morning routine for women"
- "daily affirmations for entrepreneurs"
- "stoic quotes for men"
- "bible verse of the day"
- "christian wallpaper for iphone" (low KD, high intent)
- "stoic wallpaper phone" (underserved, growing)
- "bible verse phone background" (high volume, low competition)

### Backlinks (authority lift)
Authority Score 12 with 124 referring domains is the ceiling on rankings. Target:
- Guest posts on productivity and faith blogs
- Get listed in "best wallpaper sites" roundups
- Outreach to self-improvement YouTube channels
- Target: authority score above 20 within 6 months

### Do NOT restructure the site immediately
Post-acquisition traffic dips are normal. Rankings were recovering Aug 24–29. Wholesale redesigns right after acquisition can trigger further ranking drops. Stabilize first, then improve incrementally.
